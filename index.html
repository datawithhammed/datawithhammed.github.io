Background

The analyst behind the data

I'm Adeoye Hammed, a Data & Business Analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria. I have a deep belief that every business — no matter the size — is sitting on raw data with enormous, untapped potential. My job is to find that potential, decode what the numbers are trying to say, and translate it into decisions that actually move the business forward.

I have a particular passion for Business Analytics — understanding not just what happened, but why it happened and what to do about it. Whether it's customer churn, market performance, or operational gaps, I approach every dataset with genuine curiosity and a bias toward insight.

Currently open to full-time analyst roles, freelance projects, and collaborations. I'm not limited to any single industry — if there's data, there's a story, and I want to tell it.
